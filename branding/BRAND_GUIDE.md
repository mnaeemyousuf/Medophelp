# Medophelp Brand Guide

**Medophelp** — *Smarter Healthcare. Seamless Operations.*

Single source of truth in code: `shared/src/brand.ts` (name, brand line, colours, chart order, fonts). The web theme mirrors it as CSS tokens in `client/src/index.css`. Server PDFs, report exports and the user-manual builder import it or check against it. The logo files are generated by `tools/brand/build-brand.mjs`; regenerate them instead of editing the files by hand.

## Logo

The mark is a rounded tile in a blue→teal gradient, carrying a white heartbeat line that draws the letter **M**, with a small medical cross at the upper right. It stands for care (the cross), vital signs (the pulse) and the product initial.

The wordmark is **Medophelp** in Plus Jakarta Sans ExtraBold, navy. Text in every SVG is converted to outlines, so the logo looks the same on machines without the font.

Copies served by the web app are in `client/public/`: `favicon.svg`, `favicon.ico`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, `site.webmanifest`, and `brand/*`.

**Usage rules**

* Keep clear space around the logo of at least ¼ of the mark's height.
* Minimum size: mark 16 px; horizontal logo 120 px wide.
* Don't recolour, stretch, rotate, outline or add effects. Use the reversed version on dark or busy backgrounds.
* A hospital's own name and logo (tenant data) appear *with* the Medophelp brand, never in place of it. On documents a hospital issues to patients — invoices, prescriptions, lab reports — the hospital is the issuer, and Medophelp appears as “Powered by Medophelp”.

## Typography

| Role | Typeface | Weights |
|---|---|---|
| Display — logo, headings, page titles | Plus Jakarta Sans | 700, 800 |
| Body — UI, tables, forms | Inter | 400–700 |

Both are open-source (SIL Open Font License). The web app loads them from Google Fonts. The PDF manuals embed them from `@fontsource` packages in `tools/brand`.

## Visual style

* Navy sidebar with white text; the active item is a solid blue pill.
* White cards with a 1 px slate border and a soft navy-tinted shadow, on a cool-grey page.
* Rounded corners: 6–8 px for controls and cards, 16/64 of the size for the logo tile.
* Lucide line icons at 16–20 px, in the current text colour.
* Gradient (blue→teal) only as a thin accent: logo, PDF header bars, login panel glow.

## Voice

Clear, calm, professional. Say what the system does without overstating it. Never claim regulatory compliance or clinical safety that hasn't been certified.
